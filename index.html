12 / GOOD QUESTIONSSTRAIGHT ANSWERS
BEFORE WE
GET STARTED.
A few things worth knowing about building your next project with KPM.
How much does a website cost?+
Basic websites start at R2,500, with business, animated and 3D packages priced according to scope. The quote calculator gives an indicative estimate.
How long does development take?+
Timelines depend on the number of pages, requested features and when content is provided. We'll agree on a realistic delivery plan before work begins.
Do I need to pay a deposit?+
Payment terms are agreed in the quotation or project agreement. A deposit may be requested before development starts.
Are domains and hosting included?+
Not automatically. Domain, hosting, third-party services and maintenance costs are specified separately unless the agreed quote includes them.
Can I request changes during development?+
Yes. Agreed revision rounds are included in the project scope. Additional features or major scope changes may need a revised quotation.
Does the client portal store my project information?+
Not in this public demonstration. A real portal would require private accounts, secure authentication, database permissions and a separate deployment.
Can KPM build a mobile application?+
KPM can discuss mobile-friendly websites, installable web apps and application prototypes. More complex native app work is scoped separately.
