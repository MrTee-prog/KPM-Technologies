09 / GOOD TO KNOW

REAL ANSWERS.
NO GUESSWORK.

We believe great projects start with honest conversations.

How much does a five-page website cost?+

Our Launch package starts at R2,500 for up to five pages. The final price depends on your content, features and customisation. Hosting and domain fees are separate.

Can I add animations or 3D to my website?+

Yes. We can discuss smooth interactions, animated sections or custom 3D elements. Advanced 3D is quoted separately so you only pay for what your project needs.

How long will my website take?+

Delivery depends on complexity, feedback and having the required content. After discussing your project, we'll agree on a practical timeline before work begins.

Do I need to provide images and text?+

Any existing photos, logo, descriptions and contact details help. If you need creative assistance with content, tell us during the quotation stage so it can be included in scope.

Will my website work on phones?+

Yes. Responsive design is included in our website packages. We test layouts at common mobile, tablet and desktop sizes.

What about domains, hosting and ongoing changes?+

Domain registration and paid hosting are separate costs. We can assist with setup and quote optional maintenance after launch.

Can customers pay online through my website?+

Yes, for suitable ecommerce projects we can integrate an established payment service. Fees, account verification and security requirements depend on the selected provider.
